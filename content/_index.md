Hi 👋 I am a **Member of Technical Staff (AI Research) at [Lila Sciences](https://www.lila.ai/)**, where I work on **LLM post-training and frontier capabilities** towards building **scientific superintelligence** 🔬. I am also an **Honorary Research Associate at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/)**, in the Department of Computing.

My research interests sit at the intersection of **LLMs, agentic systems, synthetic data and environments & evaluations**. In particular, I'm interested in how we develop and evaluate increasingly capable AI systems that can reason, experiment in the real world, and make scientific discoveries. A central theme throughout my work is the data-centric perspective: what we train and evaluate on fundamentally shapes what models can learn, how they generalise, and ultimately what we can trust 🦾.

Previously, I was a **Lead Research Scientist at [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/)** Foundational Research, where I led research on **LLM Evaluations** as part of the development of [**Thomson-1**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27147), Thomson Reuters' frontier-class LLM (~400B parameters).

I completed my PhD in Machine Learning at the [University of Cambridge](https://damtp.cam.ac.uk/), supervised by Prof. [Mihaela van der Schaar](https://www.vanderschaar-lab.com). My research on Data-Centric AI, Synthetic Data, Evaluations, and Large Language Models has been published at numerous top-tier ML venues including NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, AISTATS, and Nature Digital Medicine.

I also hold a Master's degree from [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/), where I worked on Bayesian Deep Learning, as well as a Master's from the [University of the Witwatersrand (South Africa)](https://www.wits.ac.za/), where I worked on Signal Processing & ML for Parkinson's Disease. I also hold a dual bachelor's in Information Engineering & Biomedical Engineering from the University of the Witwatersrand.

My previous industry experience includes time as an ML Researcher at [AstraZeneca](https://www.astrazeneca.co.uk/), working on LLM verification, test-time scaling, and uncertainty quantification. Before my PhD, I worked on production ML systems as a Data Scientist working on Computer Vision at [Shutterstock (USA)](https://www.shutterstock.com) and as an ML Engineer working on NLP at [Multichoice](https://www.multichoice.com/) (Africa's largest multimedia company).

Outside the research itself, I was named one of the Mail & Guardian's [**Top 200 Young South Africans**](https://mg.co.za/200-young-south-africans/) 🇿🇦

Always happy to chat about research — feel free to reach out!
